Vision Impairment Screening Assessment

VISA Screening Assistant

Clinician workflow • iPad optimized • local-only data

Screening aid, not diagnosis. A negative VISA screen does not rule out visual impairment. Refer to an eye specialist whenever clinical concern remains.

Patient & screen

Nothing is transmitted or stored remotely. Refresh/new screen clears entered data.

Automated interpretation

Use local referral pathways and clinical judgment. Pre-existing conditions already under eye-care follow-up may alter referral decisions when there is no post-injury deterioration/new symptom.

Reference thresholds used

  • Distance acuity: 0.0 or 0.2 in each eye = normal screening level; 0.4 or worse = reduced.
  • Near acuity: N5 or N6 in each eye = normal screening level; N8 or worse = reduced.
  • Grating: small-size grating should be achieved; large-size only = reduced.
  • Line bisection: referral threshold is more than 6 mm from center.
  • Clock cancellation: referral threshold is fewer than 42 of 50 large clocks.
  • Interactive tasks accept touch or Apple Pencil input. Clock drawing remains clinician-interpreted per the source criterion.
